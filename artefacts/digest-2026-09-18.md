## 1. How do transformer-based language models handle long context windows?

Transformer-based language models rely on an attention operation that compares each new token to every previous token, which allows them to effectively recall information but makes them increasingly inefficient as the context grows [1]. Despite being powerful, these models face a limitation with finite context windows, where performance drops or details are forgotten once the window overflows [3]. To address these challenges, researchers have developed various methodologies targeting module designs within the Transformer architecture—particularly intricate designs around the attention module—as well as optimization toolkits to enhance efficiency and efficacy across extensive context windows [2].

## 2. What were the main causes of the 2008 financial crisis?

The 2008 financial crisis was primarily caused by excessive speculation on property values by homeowners and financial institutions, which created the United States housing bubble [1, 3]. This foundation was further fueled by predatory and risky subprime mortgage lending, regulatory gaps, and Wall Street practices [1, 3]. Deficiencies in regulation and supervision acted as structural vulnerabilities that propagated and amplified the initial shocks [1, 2]. Additionally, Austrian school economists attributed the crisis partly to the Federal Reserve's prolonged policy of artificially low interest rates [1].

## 3. What is the current state of fusion energy research?

The current state of nuclear fusion research involves numerous national and international collaboration projects, such as the ITER megaproject, which uses a tokamak reactor to demonstrate magnetic confinement fusion feasibility [1]. Additionally, the National Ignition Facility (NIF) is researching methods to maximize energy output for inertial confinement [1]. While putting fusion energy on the grid is still ongoing research, it has already advanced plasma physics and technology, yielding real-world benefits like improved industrial processes and materials [2]. However, some experts caution that recent publicity regarding imminent commercial fusion energy in the next 10 to 20 years may be overly optimistic, noting it could still prove too costly to compete practically [3].

## 4. How does CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing work at a molecular level?

CRISPR gene editing is a molecular biology technique used to modify the genomes of living organisms based on a simplified bacterial antiviral defense system [1]. The process involves delivering the Cas9 nuclease complexed with a synthetic guide RNA (gRNA) directly into a cell [1]. This complex targets and cuts the cell's genome at a specific, desired location [1]. Once the cut is made, it allows existing genes to be removed or new genes to be added *in vivo* [1].
